# Reasoning Answers

## Question 1
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:
Between August 9, 2026, and August 9, 2032, there are exactly 6 years. Out of these, 2028 and 2032 are leap years, which include February 29th, while the other 4 are ordinary years.
Total odd days = $4 \times 1 \text{ (ordinary)} + 2 \times 2 \text{ (leap)} = 8$ odd days.
8 odd days mod 7 = 1 odd day.
August 9, 2026 is Sunday, so August 9, 2032 will be Sunday + 1 = Monday.
Difficulty: Medium
Concept: Calendar and Dates

## Question 4
Correct Answer: A
Explanation:
The pattern is $T_n \times 3 - (2n - 1) = T_{n+1}$.
- $4 \times 3 - 1 = 11$
- $11 \times 3 - 3 = 30$
- $30 \times 3 - 5 = 85$
- $85 \times 3 - 7 = 248$
- $248 \times 3 - 9 = 735$
Difficulty: Medium
Concept: Number Series

## Question 5
